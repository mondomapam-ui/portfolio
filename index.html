About Me

Adobe Campaign Certified Architect

With over a decade of experience in marketing technology, I've helped enterprises across Europe transform their digital capabilities. My journey began in traditional marketing, but I quickly recognized the power of technology to amplify human creativity.

As an Adobe Campaign Certified Architect, I specialize in designing and implementing complex marketing automation solutions that deliver measurable business value. My approach combines technical expertise with a deep understanding of marketing fundamentals.

I'm passionate about the intersection of technology and marketing - where data meets creativity, and automation enhances personalization. My work is guided by structured methodologies like PRINCE2® and Agile, ensuring projects are delivered on time, within scope, and with the highest quality standards.
